## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static site generator) deployed on Netlify. Chosen because the site is informational/marketing content (bio, specialties, insurance, embedded booking calendar) with no app logic or backend — Astro gives fast static pages, simple component reuse for repeated lists (specialties, methods, insurance), strong SEO defaults, and trivial embedding of the Headway booking iframe. Netlify chosen for zero-config Git deploys, a free tier that fits a small solo practice, and simple custom-domain setup.

## Users

Primary users are prospective therapy clients in New Jersey seeking virtual (teletherapy) sessions: adolescents, adults, couples, and families. Many are navigating anxiety, stress, family or relationship issues, or a difficult life transition, and are looking for a bilingual (English/Spanish), culturally competent therapist. Ages served: adolescents, adults, seniors.

## Product Purpose

A marketing/informational website for Diana Montero, LCSW, introducing her virtual therapy practice and converting visitors directly into booked sessions. The landing page must surface a prominent "Book Now" CTA linking to Headway immediately on entry, before requiring further reading.

## Positioning

A bilingual (English/Spanish), evidence-based, whole-person therapist who reduces barriers to care for diverse clients. Combines a wide range of clinical methods (see Capabilities) with a warm, calm, collaborative presence — evidence-based rigor delivered without clinical coldness.

## Operating Context

- Virtual only (teletherapy) — no physical office visits.
- Practice licensed in and serving New Jersey.
- Insurance-based practice; visitors will check accepted insurance before booking.
- Booking happens through Headway; the landing page's "Book Now" CTA links directly to Diana's Headway provider page (https://care.headway.co/providers/diana-montero-2).

## Product Principles

1. Warmth and psychological safety come before information density — this is a mental-health audience, often visiting while stressed or overwhelmed.
2. Bilingual and cultural accessibility (English/Spanish, culturally competent care) is core positioning, not a footnote.
3. The path to booking must be immediate and frictionless — the Book Now CTA is a landing-page entry element, not buried behind navigation.
4. Clinical credibility (credentials, methods, insurance) is conveyed clearly but never at the expense of a warm, human tone.
5. Content and imagery must reflect the diversity of ages, family structures, and identities she serves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Bilingual support (English/Spanish) is a confirmed, core requirement. No other accessibility standard was specified by the client; do not invent one.
